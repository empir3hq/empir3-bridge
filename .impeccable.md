## Design Context

### Users

Empir3 users watching Vincent or Colva operate their own computer; agents range from small local models to premium Astra. Users need to understand what is being observed, where input is going, and how to stop it. Tool instructions must be straightforward while retaining advanced capabilities.

### Brand Personality

Visible, capable, approachable. This comes from the owner's request for fast control that feels natural and easy to follow.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined green arrow with a clear contrasting edge, short natural travel and gentle deceleration over targets. A restrained perimeter identifies the actual observed desktop area. Labels explain observation or control without claiming a human operator. Preserve the existing Bridge typography and controls; this is control feedback rather than a site redesign.

### Design Principles

1. Show actual scope and action; never imply continuous observation when no capture is active.
2. Keep precise endpoints, bounded latency and immediate cancellation ahead of decoration.
3. Move naturally without random jitter, overshoot or repeated input.
4. Maintain contrast across arbitrary desktop content and both DPI displays.
5. Provide reduced motion and one simple observe/activate/act/verify path, with advanced tools still available.
